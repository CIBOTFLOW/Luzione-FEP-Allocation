# Sponsor Data Boundary

## Allowed sponsor data

- generalized geography;
- case category;
- broad reviewed cohort tags;
- public-safe summary;
- requested and funded amount;
- program restrictions;
- FEP-issued public case code;
- immutable approved sponsor name, tagline, and safe logo metadata;
- proof lifecycle state and generalized event time;
- aggregate outcomes above minimum cohort thresholds.

## Prohibited sponsor data

- raw application answers;
- exact diagnosis or medical documents;
- exact address or contact details;
- private identity;
- reward balance or purchase history;
- gratitude or publicity willingness;
- private fraud, verification, or appeal notes.

Logo binaries are not accepted by this reference API. The browser derives SHA-256, media type, byte size, filename, and alt text metadata; a production asset service must perform its own malware, decompression, content, and storage checks before FEP review.

Sponsors fund through allocation intents. FEP independently validates the intent, retains decision and money authority, and records the final result. The sponsor portal never receives direct approval, transfer, or recipient-contact capability.

## Two separate sponsor choices

- **Program allocation**: a sponsor expresses a preference for an approved program or a sufficiently broad reviewed cohort. A public card can never be the target.
- **Sponsored direct gift**: a sponsor may ask FEP to fund one currently published outcome by opaque public code, at the exact published amount, inside a pre-approved capped campaign. The sponsor still does not learn the private identity, choose among private applicants, contact the recipient, or impose publicity.

The second rail is availability-based and first-accepted at the FEP authority layer. A local portal hold is not an authoritative case hold, reservation, payment, or approval.

## Publication boundary

Funding consent and publication consent are distinct. Proof-feed content requires separately versioned consent and FEP review. Sponsor attribution is pinned to the approved brand version selected when the campaign was reviewed. Consent withdrawal hides the current public projection while preserving the audit and receipt trail required for accountability.

Campaign, brand, rationale, and proof copy also pass a financial-promise screen. Public content may not promise or imply cryptocurrency, a future token, conversion to cash, trading, appreciation, profit, staking, yield, an airdrop, or an investment return.
