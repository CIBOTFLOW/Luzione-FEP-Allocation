# Luzione closed-loop value boundary

## Current rule

Luzione may recognize useful participation and may later operate a tightly funded access credit, but this product must not imply that either unit is cryptocurrency, an investment, cash, stored value available for withdrawal, or a claim on future Luzione assets.

The machine-readable invariant is `src/luzioneValueBoundary.js` and is exposed to the studio through `/v1/settings`.

## Luzione Impact Points

Purpose: recognition and verified participation history.

- no monetary value;
- no transfer or P2P transfer;
- no redemption for goods, money, or credits;
- no effect on need, eligibility, priority, or appeal;
- no entitlement or promise of future conversion;
- removal or correction must be possible when source evidence is reversed.

Impact Points are not yet issued by this repository.

## Luzione Essentials Credits

Purpose: possible future access to specifically approved essential goods and services inside a closed merchant/fulfillment network.

- issuance only after cleared sponsor funds or committed fulfillment capacity exists;
- fixed accounting at issuance/redemption; no market-price mechanism;
- no transfer, P2P payment, cash-out, exchange listing, staking, yield, or appreciation claim;
- category, merchant, geography, expiration, and program restrictions must be enforceable;
- refunds, reversals, expirations, disputes, and unspent restricted balances require explicit ledger treatment;
- a recipient's need decision cannot depend on earning or holding credits.

Credits are not yet issued, spent, or settled by this repository.

## Settlement and reserves

- The sponsor funds fiat or a regulated-provider instrument.
- Restricted funds remain segregated from Luzione operating revenue.
- A recipient authorizes an approved essential purchase; a merchant or fulfillment partner is settled in fiat through an approved provider.
- Unused, reversed, or failed value follows the sponsor/program agreement and authoritative ledger rules; it is not silently recognized as Luzione revenue.
- Luzione cannot sell reserves or issue speculative units to fund FEM.

## Crypto decision gate

No public crypto language, code, repo, tokenomics, wallet, mint, smart contract, presale, exchange plan, or future-conversion promise should be created unless qualified counsel first approves a written jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction structure and regulators, payments partners, accounting, tax, sanctions, custody, consumer-protection, privacy, and money-transmission obligations are resolved.

Even if that review occurs later, current Impact Points and Essentials Credits must not create a contractual or marketing expectation of conversion. Any future system would require a new product decision and must stand on its own.

This document is an engineering/product boundary, not legal advice.
